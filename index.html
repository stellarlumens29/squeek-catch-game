How to play

← → Arrow keys A / D Mouse / Touch & drag Space = Pause
  • Every 15 seconds the level goes up — cheese falls faster.
  • A streak of 5 catches in a row earns a +20 point bonus.
  • A missed cheese breaks your combo, but doesn't cost a life.
  • Live wheel bonus: when the real Squeekchain wheel is SPINNING, Golden Rush is on and golden cheese drops far more often.
  • The game ends when time runs out or you lose all 3 lives.
